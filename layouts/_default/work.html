  • Jurassic Playground

    When the Browns first envisioned a natural and organic playground, free from the usual plastic clutter, we were thrilled to take part, though unsure of how it would unfold.

    By blending our creative vision with elements inspired by iconic films like Jurassic Park and Hook, we've crafted a truly unique space. Now, it's a place where imagination roams as freely as the children it was built for.

    Join us in a glimpse of this exclusive playground, where a menacing dinosaur helps bring our vision to life.

  • Boos

    Michelle and Allan approached us with the desire to create a “veggie garden” so that they could grow their own food. The idea started out simple and quickly grew into something more similar to a small village. The area included custom concrete planter boxes that will never rot, a greenhouse set up to grow edibles all year long, and other landscape details that really elevate the appearance of the space.

  • Church Road

    Pat and Lorne purchased a home done by a builder who saw no value in a nice landscape, despite the home being front and centre in town. The quality of the landscape was disappointing, given that it was part of a brand new built home. They worked with our designer to build a design that gave their home the curb appeal it deserved without adding any maintenance. As you can see, the results were wonderful!
